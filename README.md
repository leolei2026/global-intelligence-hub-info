# Global Intelligence Hub

**AI-Powered Consumer Insights & Market Research Platform**

## 1. Project Overview

Global Intelligence Hub is an early-stage AI-powered consumer insights and market research project developed by an independent developer.

The project aims to identify consumer pain points, unmet needs, product feedback, and emerging market opportunities by analyzing online discussions and product-related information.

The platform is currently under development and is not operated by a registered company.

## 2. Core Capabilities

The planned platform includes:

- Collection of data from authorized sources
- Data cleaning, deduplication, and relevance screening
- AI-powered analysis of consumer feedback
- Classification of product complaints and unmet needs
- Identification of emerging product opportunities
- Aggregated market research reports

## 3. Target Markets

Initial research interests include:

- Cycling and micromobility accessories
- Smart pet products
- RV and outdoor accessories
- Consumer electronics and smart devices

The platform is designed to support product research and development decisions.

## 4. Intended Reddit Data Usage

Global Intelligence Hub is seeking appropriate authorization to access Reddit data for potential commercial market research.

If authorized, the project intends to analyze relevant Reddit posts and comments to identify aggregated consumer trends, product complaints, purchasing considerations, and unmet needs.

Reddit data access and processing will be subject to Reddit's applicable agreements, policies, and approved usage scope.

The project does not currently operate an automated Reddit data collection pipeline.

## 5. Privacy and Responsible Data Use

The project is intended to focus on aggregated consumer insights rather than individual user profiling.

The developer intends to:

- Use Reddit data only within the scope of applicable authorization
- Avoid selling or redistributing raw Reddit data
- Avoid building individual user profiles
- Apply appropriate data retention and deletion practices
- Respect applicable privacy requirements and platform policies

## 6. Development Status

**Status: Early-stage development**

The core data processing and AI analysis workflow is being developed and tested.

Reddit integration is pending appropriate data access authorization.

## 7. Project Ownership

**Developer:** Independent Developer

**Project:** Global Intelligence Hub

**Business Status:** Independent project; no registered company currently operates this platform.

**Purpose:** AI-powered consumer insights, product opportunity discovery, and market research.
